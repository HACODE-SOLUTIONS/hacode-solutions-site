# RECAP EMAIL — Same-day “Heard you”

Send within a few hours of the call (same day ET). Purpose: prove you listened, park soft scope, set the brief + quote path—**not** to deliver free consulting.

Replace bracket tokens. Keep under ~200 words unless they asked for detail.

---

## Script A — Standard (decision-maker present)

**Subject:** Heard you — [Project nickname] scope next

```text
Hi [First name],

Thanks for the call today on [Project nickname]. Here’s what I heard:

• Trigger: [one line]
• Success (near-term): [30/60-day outcome in one line]
• v1 In: [A], [B], [C]
• v1 Out (for now): [D], [E] — including soft polish / “maybe” items until we write them In
• Constraints: budget band [X–Y] · hard date [date or none] · stack notes [short]

Open questions (≤3) before I quote:
1) [Q1]
2) [Q2]
3) [Q3]

I’ll send a one-page scope brief by [time/date ET] for your review. Once you confirm
the brief (and that [Decision-maker] owns scope), I’ll send a quote / commercial next
step—not before.

Reply on this thread with corrections. If I missed something critical, say so in one
bullet—don’t restart the whole product in email.

Talk soon,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Script B — Decision-maker missing

**Subject:** Heard you — need [Name] before we lock scope

```text
Hi [First name],

Appreciate today. Captured trigger + rough In/Out for [Project nickname].

I can’t lock a fair scope brief or quote until [Decision-maker role/name] confirms
scope ownership (even async). Could you loop them on this thread or book 20 minutes
with both of us?

Meanwhile I’m holding: In = [A/B/C]; Out = [soft items]. I’ll draft the brief after
confirmation—not a free redesign email thread.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Script C — Soft-scope / free-consulting pressure

**Subject:** Recap + boundary — [Project nickname]

```text
Hi [First name],

Quick recap so we don’t lose the thread:

Heard you want [outcome]. For a paid v1 I’d keep In: [A/B]. I’m parking [make it pop /
AI somewhere / mobile / payments-maybe] as Out until we write them as scoped line items.

I’m happy to send a locked scope brief + quote. I’m not able to keep iterating
architecture for free on email—happy to do a paid discovery spike if you want deeper
options analysis first.

Which path: (1) brief + quote on the In list above, or (2) paid discovery spike?

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Tokens cheat sheet

| Token | From |
|-------|------|
| `[Project nickname]` | Short name you both used |
| `[Decision-maker]` | Named human for scope |
| In / Out lists | `LIVE-NOTES.md` §5–6 |
| Open questions | Cap at 3 from notes |

---

## Do / Don’t

**Do:** Send same day. Mirror their words for trigger + success. Park soft asks Out.  
**Don’t:** Attach a full proposal essay. Invent budget numbers. Promise “scope locked” without a named decision-maker.
