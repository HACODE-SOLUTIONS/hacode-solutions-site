# FOLLOW-UP EMAILS — Send brief · nudge · close/lose

Three emails. Do not invent a 12-touch nurture sequence—this kit is close ops, not a CRM course.

Replace tokens from `SCOPE-BRIEF.md` / `LIVE-NOTES.md`.

---

## Email 1 — Send brief

**When:** Same day or next morning after recap (when brief is ready).  
**Subject:** Scope brief for [Project nickname] — please confirm

```text
Hi [First name],

Attached / below is the scope brief for [Project nickname] based on our call.

Outcome: [one sentence]
In: [A], [B], [C]
Out: [D], [E]
Open questions (≤3): [list or “none — ready to quote”]

Please reply “confirmed” or send redlines as bullets. Once [Decision-maker] confirms,
I’ll send the quote (deposit [amount or “with quote”]) so we can start.

I’m not expanding scope over email—if something big is missing, say so and we’ll
decide In vs paid change vs Out.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Email 2 — Nudge

**When:** 3–5 business days after Email 1 with no reply (or after they said “next week”).  
**Subject:** Re: Scope brief for [Project nickname]

```text
Hi [First name],

Checking in on the scope brief for [Project nickname]. Happy to jump on 15 minutes
if a redline is stuck—otherwise a “confirmed” or “not moving forward” keeps us honest.

Quote remains lined up for [validity note, e.g. through DATE]. After that I’ll assume
it’s closed on our side so I can free the start window.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Email 3 — Close / lose

**When:** After nudge + still silence, or clear “not now.” Preserve the relationship; free your calendar.  
**Subject:** Closing the loop — [Project nickname]

```text
Hi [First name],

I’m closing the loop on [Project nickname] so neither of us carries a ghost thread.

I’ll mark this as not moving forward for now. If priorities change, reply with what’s
different and we can refresh the brief (scope ages—don’t assume the old quote still
holds).

Appreciate the conversation,
[Your Name]
```

### Variant — polite lose when they want free forever

```text
Hi [First name],

Thanks again for the interest. I’m not able to keep iterating plans unpaid, so I’m
closing this thread. If you’d like a paid discovery spike or to confirm the existing
brief and move to a quote, I’m here—otherwise I’ll assume we’re done for now.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Cadence cheat sheet

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 0 (call day) | Recap (`RECAP-EMAIL.md`) |
| 0–1 | Email 1 — Send brief |
| +3–5 business days | Email 2 — Nudge |
| +7–10 business days | Email 3 — Close/lose |

If they engage with redlines, pause the cadence and work the brief—don’t send close/lose mid-negotiation.
