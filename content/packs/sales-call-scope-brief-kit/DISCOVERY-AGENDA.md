# DISCOVERY AGENDA — Timeboxed sales / discovery call

**Use before and during the call.** Goal: leave with enough signal to draft a locked scope brief the same day—not a free architecture consult.

**Default length:** 30 minutes (stretch to 45 only if they brought the decision-maker and a hard deadline).

---

## Pre-call checklist (5 minutes)

- [ ] Confirm **who is on the call** and who can say yes to budget + scope.
- [ ] Open `LIVE-NOTES.md` (blank copy for this prospect).
- [ ] Skim any form answers / email thread (do not rebuild their product in your head yet).
- [ ] Decide your stop rule: if no decision-maker after 10 minutes, end early and reschedule.

### Decision-maker check (hard gate)

| Question | Pass | Fail |
|----------|------|------|
| Who can approve scope changes? | Named human on this call (or joining in ≤48h) | “We’ll loop someone in later” with no name |
| Who can approve budget / deposit? | Named (can be same person) | Vague “finance” / “the team” |
| Timeline owner | Someone who owns the go-live date | “Whenever” with no urgency and no blocker |

If fail: use the **reschedule script** at the bottom. Do not give away a free mini-proposal.

---

## 30-minute agenda

| Min | Block | You ask / do | Capture in LIVE-NOTES |
|-----|-------|--------------|------------------------|
| 0–3 | Open | “Goal for today: confirm the outcome, what’s in/out for v1, and whether we’re the right fit to quote.” Confirm recording OK if you use one. | — |
| 3–8 | Trigger | “What changed that made this urgent now?” | Trigger |
| 8–15 | Success | “What does good look like in 30 / 60 / 90 days?” | Success 30/60/90 |
| 15–20 | Current state | “What exists today? Tools, half-builds, people touching it?” | Current state |
| 20–24 | Constraints | Budget band (range OK), hard dates, stack preferences, compliance. | Constraints |
| 24–27 | In / Out | Propose a sharp In list; park soft asks (“make it pop”, “maybe Stripe”) as Out until written In. | In / Out |
| 27–30 | Next step | Confirm decision-maker, open questions ≤3, and same-day recap + brief ETA. | Next step |

### Stretch to 45 minutes (only if…)

Only add 15 minutes when: decision-maker is present **and** they need to walk a demo/Figma/repo. Extra time is for **their** artifacts, not your consulting monologue.

---

## Questions that earn their keep (pick, don’t spray)

**Trigger**
- What broke or got expensive enough to take this call?
- What happens if you do nothing for 90 days?

**Success**
- Who notices when this works (users, ops, revenue)?
- What metric or demo proves v1 worked?

**Current state**
- What’s already built / bought? What failed before?
- Who will own the product after handoff?

**Constraints**
- Fixed price vs weekly retainer preference?
- Hard date: event, pilot, board, season?
- Must-use tools (Notion, Stripe, Auth.js, existing repo)?

**In / Out (say out loud)**
- “For v1 I’m hearing In: A, B, C. Out unless we expand: D, E. Correct?”
- Soft phrases (“make it pop”, “AI somewhere”, “mobile later”) → Out by default.

---

## What you will not do on this call

- Free architecture redesign spanning their whole stack.
- Live pricing theater without constraints captured.
- Promise “we’ll figure scope later” after a deposit.
- Write lawyer MSA language on the spot.

## Reschedule script (no decision-maker)

```text
Subject: Quick reschedule — need [Name] on the discovery call

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the time today. To quote accurately I need the person who can lock
scope and budget on the call (even 20 minutes). Who should we include, and what
two times work this week?

I’ll send a short agenda so we stay on outcome + in/out—not a free rebuild.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

## After hang-up (same day)

1. Finish `LIVE-NOTES.md` within 15 minutes while memory is fresh.  
2. Run the skill (`SKILL.md`) → draft `SCOPE-BRIEF.md`.  
3. Send `RECAP-EMAIL.md` (“Heard you”) same day.  
4. Only then open `PRICING-WORKSHEET.md` / `QUOTE-SOW-CHECKLIST.md`.
