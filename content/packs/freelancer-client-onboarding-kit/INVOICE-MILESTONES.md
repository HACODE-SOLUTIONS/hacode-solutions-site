## Late payment operating notes (non-legal)

Defaults you may adopt if your agreement allows—**confirm against your contract**:

| Topic | Suggested operating practice |
|-------|------------------------------|
| Reminder | Friendly nudge at due date + 3 business days |

| Access | Do not revoke client-owned accounts punitively; pause *your* unpaid labor |
