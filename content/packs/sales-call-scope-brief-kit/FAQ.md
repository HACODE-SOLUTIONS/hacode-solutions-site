## Common footguns

### 1. Free consulting trap

Prospect says “send us some ideas.” You write a free mini-proposal. They ghost—or use your ideas with someone cheaper.

**Fix:** Same-day `RECAP-EMAIL.md` with In/Out + ≤3 questions. Script C if they keep milking. Offer paid discovery spike or stop.

### 2. Missing decision-maker

Office manager “runs day to day” but owner signs anything over $2k. You “lock” scope with the wrong human.

**Fix:** `GAP: decision-maker` on notes/brief; refuse lock language; Script B reschedule / loop-in.

### 3. Soft “make it pop”

Undefined polish lands inside a fixed bid and destroys margin.

**Fix:** Default to **Out**. Require written In + price impact on `PRICING-WORKSHEET.md`.

### 4. Maybe payments / Stripe

“If it’s easy” becomes unpaid Auth+webhook work.

**Fix:** Default **Out**. If moved In, compose Product #1 (`mvp-auth-stripe-billing`, $49) and price the hours.

### 5. Treating this kit as Product #2

You try to run kickoff, access owners, and invoice milestones from the close pack.

**Fix:** `HANDOFF-TO-PRODUCT2.md` after deposit — Freelancer Client Onboarding Kit ($39).

### 6. Quote before brief

Fixed price on foggy scope.

**Fix:** `SCOPE-BRIEF.md` → `QUOTE-SOW-CHECKLIST.md` send gate.

### 7. More than three open questions

Endless discovery email.

**Fix:** Cap ≤3 on the brief; park the rest or sell a spike.

### 8. Three homes for the truth

Notion + Docs + Markdown copies drift.

**Fix:** Pick Path A, B, or C in `START-HERE.md` once per prospect.
